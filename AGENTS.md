## Conventions

- Never write tests. The project has no test suite, no test task, and no `*_test.odin` files.
- Never write comments. This covers the Odin sources, the Makefile, and scripts. Knowledge that would have been a comment belongs in this file or in the commit message.

## Build and run

- Odin port of the raylib C quickstart. `src/main.c` is gone; no C source remains.
- `make` builds the debug binary into `bin/debug/`; `make release`, `make run`, `make clean` also work. See the Makefile.
- raylib comes from the Odin toolchain bindings (`import rl "vendor:raylib"`, plus `vendor:raylib/rlgl` in `economy` and `planet_visuals`), which link the prebuilt static lib under `$ODIN_ROOT/vendor/raylib/<platform>/`. Raygui is not used; the inspector is hand-drawn rectangles.
- The display font is Michroma (SIL OFL, license beside it in `assets/fonts/`), compiled into the binary with `#load`, so no runtime asset path exists. `odin check src -vet-unused` is clean; keep it that way.
- macOS: the vendor `libraylib.a` symlink resolves through Homebrew's `/opt/homebrew/opt/raylib`; `libraygui-arm64.a` must link before `libraylib.a`, which Odin does automatically.
- Windows: Odin links the prebuilt `vendor/raylib/windows/raylib.lib` and `raygui.lib`; `odin` must be on PATH (for example inside the w64devkit terminal).
- DANGER: never run `premake5 gmake` or the old `build/*.bat` regenerate step. It overwrites the hand-written Odin `Makefile` with the C/C++ quickstart makefiles, after which `make` fails with `undefined reference to WinMain` (there is no C entry point). `build-MinGW-W64.bat` is already fixed to just run `make`.

## World and starting state

- Eight planets in solar order, indexed by named constants (`MERCURY` 0 through `NEPTUNE` 7, `EARTH` 2, `PLANET_COUNT` 8). Positions and radii are the `planets` table in `main.odin`: Mercury {-30, 2, -2} r1.6, Venus {-15, 0.8, -8} r2.6, Earth {0, 0, 0} r3.0, Mars {22, 1, 6} r2.2, Jupiter {50, 2.5, -12} r4.2, Saturn {-25, 3, 18} r3.8, Uranus {5, 4, -20} r3.0, Neptune {35, 5, 24} r2.9.
- The X spread is deliberate and does not follow solar order for the outer planets: the outer three were shifted left so Earth sits near mid-pack. Saturn is left of Venus (x -25, z 18), Uranus x 5, Neptune x 35, and Jupiter x 50 bounds the right edge.
- The enemy HQ is a sector, not a planet: `ENEMY_HOME` is `PLANET_COUNT` and `SECTOR_COUNT` is `PLANET_COUNT + 1`. `ENEMY_HQ_POSITION` is {140, 5, 24}, Neptune's original orbit, far off to the right by design. `sector_pos` / `sector_radius` return the HQ for `ENEMY_HOME`, and the combat, timer, and orbital-defense arrays are sized `[SECTOR_COUNT]`, so `update_planet_combat`, `planet_combatants`, `kill_fighter`, and `update_orbital_defenses` all work on the HQ with no special casing.
- Occupation ladder, indexed by sector (`GARRISON_FIGHTERS`, `GARRISON_MINERS`, `GARRISON_BASE_HP` in `main.odin`): Mercury 30/8/20, Venus 15/4/10, Earth 0/0/0, Mars 20/6/15, Jupiter 75/10/30, Saturn 60/14/40, Uranus 45/18/50, Neptune 95/22/60, HQ 500/0/500. The ladder does not follow distance from Earth or solar order.
- `initialize_game` gives Earth `STARTING_EARTH_BASES` 1 base, `STARTING_EARTH_ORBITAL_DEFENSE` 1 battery, `STARTING_EARTH_REFINERY` true, `STARTING_EARTH_MINERS` 5 mining drones, `STARTING_EARTH_COMBAT` 0 fighters, and `minerals` 350, and gives the HQ `STARTING_HQ_ORBITAL_DEFENSE` 10 (1000 HP). Every other planet starts occupied with its garrison. Standing garrisons total roughly 420 drones planetside plus 500 at the HQ, so `MAX_UNITS` 2560 leaves room.
- `reset_world` (in `simulation.odin`) is the full teardown; it also clears `drone_speed_level`, `earth_rally`, `auto_build_mode`, `victory`, and `game_paused`.

## Economy

- Income is continuous, not per round trip. `update_mining_income` accumulates `global_mps()` into `mineral_accumulator` every tick and pays out whole minerals. `planet_mps(p)` is `min(player miners targeting p, planet_mining_cap(p)) * mining_rate(p) / planet_cycle_time(p)`.
- `planet_cycle_time(p)` is `MINING_DURATION` 4.0 plus `DEPOSIT_DURATION` 0.5 plus `2 * dist(p, Earth) / MINING_TRANSIT_SPEED` (`MINING_TRANSIT_SPEED` 1.3125). That is where the historical "4.5 + 2 * dist / 1.3125" divisor comes from.
- `mining_rate` is 10 for Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars and 25 for Jupiter and beyond. `planet_mining_cap`: Mercury 15, Venus 35, Earth 10, Mars 50, Jupiter 100, Saturn 90, Uranus 60, Neptune 60.
- Working a cap: `update_miner` only enters `.MINING` while `active_mining_drones_count(p)` is under the cap, so surplus drones sit `.IDLE` in orbit as a zero-wait reserve and step up when an active miner dies or is pulled into construction. Income counts non-`.CONSTRUCTING` player miners targeted at the planet, capped at `planet_mining_cap`, so idle surplus never over-earns.
- A planet without an operational refinery earns nothing: `planet_can_mine(p)` gates `planet_mps`, `global_mps`, and `is_effective_miner`. Once a refinery is up the planet is tethered to Earth by a beam (`planet_has_refinery_beam`, `draw_refinery_mineral_beams` in `economy.odin`).
- Drones never ferry minerals. Nothing ever assigns `.RETURNING`, so the `.RETURNING` / `.DEPOSITING` half of `update_miner` and the deposit inside `is_effective_miner` are unreachable dead machinery. Do not build on them without reviving the transition first.
- Refinery: cost `planet_mining_cap(p) * 10`, `REFINERY_BUILD_TIME` 60s, crew of `REFINERY_CONSTRUCT_MINERS` 10 on site. Available and arriving miners auto-join as `.CONSTRUCTING` (`refinery_constructing_miners`), the clock only runs with a full crew, and the crew returns to `.MINING` on completion. Start it from the outpost inspector or with `B`.
- Base construction: Earth only, `BASE_COST` 500, gated at `base_counts[EARTH] < MAX_BASES` 5. It queues immediately and needs `BASE_CONSTRUCT_MINERS` 5 crew for `BASE_CONSTRUCT_TIME` 60s. Recruitment prefers Earth-assigned miners (`earth_assigned_miner_available`); a foreign-route miner only soaks into the crew once no Earth-assigned miner remains. When a base finishes, `fill_production_lines(p)` immediately pulls the next pending item, so a freshly built base starts the next queued unit with no re-queue.
- Production: each base is one parallel line. Base times are `MINER_BUILD_TIME` 4.0 and `COMBAT_BUILD_TIME` 8.0, scaled by `drone_build_time(kind)` = base * `math.pow(DRONE_SPEED_UPGRADE_FACTOR` 0.8`, drone_speed_level)`. Queue capacity is `base_counts * 5` with `MAX_PENDING` 25 overflow.
- Costs: `MINER_COST` 50, `COMBAT_COST` 125, `BATCH_MINER_COST` 250, `BATCH_COMBAT_COST` 625 for `BATCH_BUILD_COUNT` 5.
- Build-speed upgrade: `purchase_drone_speed_upgrade`, Earth only, `U` or the inspector button, `DRONE_SPEED_UPGRADE_COST` 5000 per level, `DRONE_SPEED_UPGRADE_MAX` 5. It compounds to roughly a third of the base build time. The dock shows the label `DRONE SPEED` with `LVL n/5` and a segmented meter. `drone_speed_maxed()` hides the button.
- Batch orders: the `[N] +5 MINERS` and `[X] +5 COMBAT` buttons (`queue_5_miner_button_rect`, `queue_5_combat_button_rect`) appear only at `base_counts[EARTH] >= MAX_BASES`; `N` and `X` queue them directly. The single-build keys are `M` and `C`.
- Autobuilder: `T` toggles it (`toggle_autobuild_mode`), also gated on `base_counts[EARTH] >= MAX_BASES` (`can_use_autobuilder`). `Auto_Build_Mode` is `OFF`, `MINERS`, or `COMBAT`; the queue header draws a radio (`autobuild_radio_rects`) and `auto_build_last_mode` remembers the last active mode. `update_autobuilder` queues a batch of 5 when nothing is queued and it is affordable, otherwise retries on a 1s timer (`autobuild_retry_timer`).
- Construction order: several projects can be in flight (base, refinery, orbital defense, ammo) and `planet_next_needed_build_target(p)` plus `construction_seq_counter` decide who the available miners join. Crews transfer on completion.
- Cancelling: `ESC` cancels the newest queued build with a full refund (`cancel_last_queued`); clicking a queue slot cancels that slot (`cancel_queued_at`), acting on active lines in base order then pending items, and cancelling a line promotes the first pending item. Slot rects come from `queue_slot_rect`, shared by rendering and hit-testing.

## Orbital defenses

- One battery per sector. Player batteries are always Level 1: `can_build_orbital_defense` refuses when `orbital_defense_level > 0`, and completion sets the level to 1. `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_MAX_LEVEL` 10 is unreachable on player worlds and the planet battery model has no per-level variants; only the HQ runs at Level 10. Do not describe player batteries as upgradable.
- Build cost `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_COST` 1000, `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_BUILD_TIME` 60s, crew `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_CONSTRUCT_MINERS` 10 (on Earth it additionally requires 10 miners on Earth). HP is `orbital_defense_level * ORBITAL_DEFENSE_HP_PER_LEVEL` (100 per level; `orbital_defense_max_hp`). Earth starts with one; liberated outposts can build theirs before or concurrently with a refinery. Inspector button or `O`.
- Ammo: `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_AMMO_MAX` 10 and `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_START_AMMO` 5 to start (the HQ starts full at 10). Buying a round costs `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_AMMO_COST` 200, takes `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_AMMO_BUILD_TIME` 20s, and occupies `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_AMMO_CONSTRUCT_MINERS` 1 miner. Buy from the conquered-planet overlay `+` button or the inspector ammo row. One round kills up to `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_KILLS_PER_SHOT` 10 in-transit fighters. An empty battery holds fire, and the overlay renders `0/10` in red.
- The HQ uses the other firing path: up to `orbital_defense_level * ORBITAL_DEFENSE_KILLS_PER_LEVEL` (100 at Level 10) kills per wave, with a hardcoded 0.06s fire timer and no ammo. `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_FIRE_COOLDOWN` 0.5 exists but is unused; player batteries dump a whole volley in one tick.
- Targeting (`update_orbital_defenses`): only approaching enemy fighters in `.TRANSIT`, never ones already `.GUARDING` in orbit, and only inside `sector_radius + ORBITAL_DEFENSE_TARGET_RANGE` 6.0. The turret slews with `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_TRACK_SPEED` 3.8 azimuth and `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_ELEV_SPEED` 2.4 elevation, resumes its scan sweep at `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_SCAN_SPEED` 0.8, and the per-wave kill quota and ammo settle after `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_WAVE_RESET_DELAY` 10s with nothing inbound. Blasts spawn at the muzzle (`orbital_defense_muzzle_pos_toward`) and draw via `spawn_orbital_defense_blast` / `draw_orbital_defense_blasts` (`MAX_ORBITAL_BLASTS` 32).
- Building or upgrading lowers the gun into a maintenance cradle and it cannot fire (`orbital_defense_building[p]`); the inspector shows `UPGRADE LVL n (DISABLED)` and `draw_ambulance_hazard_lights` flashes.
- Siege order at a sector: orbiting fighters engage the guarding defenders first; once the defenders are gone they attack the battery, then the base. On a planet, losing the battery destroys the refinery too.

## Enemy attacks

- `update_enemy_waves` (in `combat.odin`) runs every tick from `step_simulation`, so wave clocks and all dogfight attrition only advance during unpaused play. It calls `update_combat_vision`, `update_orbital_defenses`, `update_minor_wave`, `update_wave`, then `update_planet_combat` for every sector.
- Minor waves (`update_minor_wave`, `launch_minor_wave`, `collect_minor_wave_attacks`): every `MINOR_WAVE_INTERVAL` 75s, independent of mined worlds and the main wave clock. Liberation pairing is greedy by distance: each not-yet-liberated planet attacks at most one liberated planet, its closest available target, and each liberated planet is attacked by at most one occupier, so `min(liberated, occupied)` concurrent waves run. `closest_liberated_planet_to` prefers an off-world planet but can pick Earth, and `minor_wave_warning_planet` prefers Earth. Each pair launches `MINOR_WAVE_SIZE` 10 fighters in a tight wedge from the source. No minor waves launch once every planet is liberated.
- Minor-wave warnings: 3s before launch the target glows red at half battle intensity (`planet_is_minor_wave_target`, `minor_wave_warning_planet`) and the source glows amber (`planet_is_minor_wave_source`, `minor_wave_source_nebula_intensity` 0.5), fading after launch. The target's glare does not stop at launch: it persists while the drones are in transit (`planet_under_attack_warning`, `combat_nebula_intensity` 0.5) and hands off smoothly to the full battle glare (1.0) when they arrive.
- Waves (`update_wave`, `launch_wave`): first at `WAVE_FIRST_DELAY` 180s, then every `WAVE_INTERVAL` 180s, and only while `liberated_planet_count() >= WAVE_MIN_LIBERATED_PLANETS` 2. `attack_wave_size()` is (liberated planets - 1) * `WAVE_FIGHTERS_PER_LIBERATED` 15, so three liberated worlds send 30. The fighters spawn in a ring around the HQ (`spawn_n_enemies_to`) and strike `closest_liberated_planet_to_hq()`.
- HQ mustering: while the player presses the HQ (`player_attacking_hq`, stationed or inbound) and its defenders are below `ENEMY_HQ_GARRISON` 500, `launch_wave` sends the wave to `spawn_hq_defenders` instead. Planet attacks resume once the garrison is whole. A destroyed HQ launches nothing.
- `WAVE_MIN_MINING_PLANETS` 2 exists in `types.odin` but is unused. The live gate is `WAVE_MIN_LIBERATED_PLANETS`.
- SHARP EDGE: `launch_wave` zeroes `enemy_wave_timer` and sets `wave_started` before it checks `size <= 0 || enemy_hq_destroyed()`, so a null launch still resets the cadence.
- Every enemy spawn clamps to remaining capacity with `min(count, MAX_UNITS - unit_count)`.
- `Ctrl+N` forces a wave immediately (debug affordance).

## Combat resolution

- `update_planet_combat` ticks at `combat_tick_for(p)`: `COMBAT_TICK` 0.2 at planets, `HQ_COMBAT_TICK` (`COMBAT_TICK` / 5, 0.04) at `ENEMY_HOME`. The aliases `COMBAT_TICK_HQ`, `ENEMY_HQ_COMBAT_TICK`, and `COMBAT_TICK_ENEMY_HQ` all equal the HQ tick. With fighters guarding on both sides, one drone per side dies per tick, so a 5v5 at a planet ends in about a second.
- With no player defenders left, the tick kills a player miner (`kill_player_miner`) per tick. Exception: if the sector still has an active battery, the tick drains the battery's HP instead and the miners survive.
- `.TRANSIT` miners are never killed by `kill_player_miner`, so transit is safe, and a pinned scout gets `SCOUT_SURVIVAL` 3.5s grace before it becomes a valid garrison target.
- A planet is liberated when its base HP reaches 0, which the rules only allow after every enemy drone there is dead. At the HQ, fighters must breach the Level-10 battery (1000 HP) before `enemy_base_hp[ENEMY_HOME]` (500) can be reduced; a defeated battery slumps into a disabled wreck.
- Earth siege: with enemies holding Earth and no player fighters or miners left there (`player_miners_at` ignores transit, mirroring `kill_player_miner`), `base_timer[EARTH]` drops one command base every `BASE_SIEGE_TIME` 2.0s via `destroy_player_base`. That clears the freed production line and re-runs `fill_production_lines`; a line above the new cap stays out of service and pending items wait rather than promote into it.
- Defeat (`defeat_condition`) latches `defeated` when the player has zero units of any kind (enemy garrisons and waves occupy unit slots and must not block it) and zero bases map-wide. Victory (`victory_achieved`) latches `victory` when every planet is liberated and `enemy_hq_destroyed()`. Both freeze the simulation and show an overlay that shares the same button rects and keys.

## Vision and intel

- Fog is presence-based but not unit-only. `has_vision(p)` (in `simulation.odin`) is true for Earth, for `COMBAT_VISION_LINGER` 3.0s after the last player fighter there dies (`combat_vision_timer`), for any sector holding a player structure (`has_player_structure`: refinery, battery, or command base), or for any non-enemy unit within `sector_radius + 2.0` (squared-distance test). Structures keep a sector lit, so "retreat the last unit and it goes dark" is not always true.
- Enemy units at a dark sector are fully concealed in 3D (`is_concealed`) and the outpost inspector shows `UNSCOUTED`.
- `update_intel` runs per tick from `step_simulation` while a sector is lit and snapshots enemy fighters, miners, base HP, and up to `INTEL_UNIT_CAP` 200 per-unit records (kind, state, enemy) into `last_known_intel[p]`.
- Once a scouted sector goes dark the inspector renders a dimmed GHOST view (`ghost_view`, `draw_ghost_rosters`): the normal roster sections from the frozen snapshot, headers suffixed `[STALE]`, an amber stale-intel stronghold card, and a translucent overlay. Tile clicks and box select are disabled under ghost. A never-scouted sector stays `UNSCOUTED`. `view_count` keeps headers and hitboxes in agreement under ghost.

## UI

- Theme is a Star Fox Zero style green holo HUD (`SCIFI_*` in `types.odin`). The primary accent is `SCIFI_GREEN` #47FCAF (player fire, borders, focus), with `SCIFI_MINT` #B9EFD7 for bright text, `SCIFI_LABEL` for captions, `SCIFI_FILL` for focused fills, and `SCIFI_INK` for dark text on those fills. `SCIFI_CYAN` #28D7FF is reserved for the planet selection reticle. Amber, red, and blue keep their gameplay meanings. Primitives in `ui_primitives.odin`: `draw_winged_panel_left` / `draw_winged_panel_right`, `draw_fighter_vector_icon`, `draw_tactical_container`, `draw_section_header`, `draw_chamfered_panel`, `draw_corner_brackets`, `draw_segmented_meter`, and `draw_button`.
- Fonts: the display font (Michroma) is used for the menus, the resource HUD, and planet labels; the inspector, controls body text, and overlay badges still use raylib's default pixel font. `display_font` lazily loads and caches one atlas per physical pixel size (logical size times `GetWindowScaleDPI`), so text stays crisp at 125% scaling.
- The whole UI sits on an 8px-grid layout (`SCREEN_PANEL_WIDTH` 330, `PANEL_PAD_X` 20, `PANEL_CONTENT_W` = `SCREEN_PANEL_WIDTH` - 2 * `PANEL_PAD_X`, section metrics in `types.odin`). Render procs and click hitboxes share the same rect helpers (`queue_slot_rect`, `unit_tile_rect`, `orbital_defense_button_rect`, `conquered_planet_overlay_ammo_btn_rect`), so they cannot drift.
- Top-left resource HUD is `draw_resource_hud` (in `ui_hud.odin`, `RESOURCE_HUD_W` x `RESOURCE_HUD_H`), modeled on Star Fox Zero's "TOTAL HITS" counter: a `RESOURCES` top rail, a large glowing mineral count, `MINERALS` with `+n / S` beside it, a bottom rail that rises into a slash, and a `DRONE SPEED LVL n/5` sub-rail with a segmented meter. Digits are drawn one per fixed-pitch cell, so the count never wobbles; the side label only moves when the digit count changes (minimum 3 cells). FPS, altitude, and zoom telemetry sit bottom-right of the viewport, left of the inspector.
- `draw_squad_hud` (in `ui_hud.odin`) renders compact `[n:count]` pills at `BADGE_Y`, just below the resource HUD. Squads: `Shift+1..9` saves the selection (`save_squad`), bare `1..9` recalls it (`recall_squad`). Membership lives on `Unit.squad` (0 = none), so it self-prunes as units die or are reassigned.
- The `Controls` button (`controls_button_rect`, `BOTTOM_DOCK_H` 36) opens `draw_controls_overlay`, a full-screen directive modal that pauses the simulation. Its rows include F11 fullscreen, `T` autobuild, `ESC` cancel build, click-slot cancel, and the fog / ghost notes.
- Inspector (`ui_inspector.odin`): `draw_inspector` picks the Earth, HQ, or outpost variant. Earth shows the command-base button (hidden at the cap by `base_button_visible()`), the orbital-defense section, and `FLEET REQUISITION` with `[M] MINER` / `[C] COMBAT`, the `[N]` / `[X]` batches at 5 bases, `[U] DRONE BUILD SPEED`, then `QUEUE BUFFER` with the autobuild radio and clickable slots. Outposts show a status card (`ENEMY STRONGHOLD` with fighters and base HP, an amber `STALE INTEL` card, or `UNSCOUTED SECTOR`), then refinery, battery, and rosters. The HQ shows `HOSTILE CITADEL` with garrison, defense level, and an HP bar, or `CITADEL SILENCED - HUSK`, or `UNSCOUTED REACHES`.
- The Earth inspector positions its sections from helper procs (`production_title_y`, `production_first_y`, `production_orders_y`) that hang off `earth_orbital_defense_bottom_y()`, and `earth_orbital_defense_y()` returns `SECTION_TOP` when the base button is hidden, so the block closes up and no empty gap remains. `BASE_COLLAPSE_Y` is dead.
- Rosters: player `MINING DRONES (n/cap)` and `FIGHTING DRONES (n)`; enemy `HOSTILE MINING (n)` and `HOSTILE FIGHTERS (n)`, drawn only while lit and non-empty. Tiles are `TILE_SIZE` 26, `TILE_GAP` 4 (pitch 30), `TILES_PER_ROW` 10, drawn with no box via `draw_unit_icon`: a white wireframe of the actual drone mesh, baked once in `init_drone_visuals` by `bake_drone_icon` (projected edges, coplanar diagonals dropped, depth-faded) into `drone_icons` and tinted amber (miner), blue (fighter), or red (hostile), plus a corner dot colored by `state_color`; selection shows cyan corner brackets only. Roster geometry: `ROSTER_BASE_Y` 150, `ROSTER_BELOW_QUEUE` 206, `QUEUE_DY` 108. The bottom strip shows `ACTIVE SQUAD: n UNITS SELECTED` or `NO UNITS SELECTED`.
- Selection and orders: spacebar jumps to Earth (`select_earth`), and pressing it again centers the camera (`center_camera_on_earth`). Right-click is `handle_planet_right_click`: with a selection it issues a move order; with none and Earth selected it sets or clears the Earth rally flag (`earth_rally`, sentinel `NO_RALLY` -1; right-clicking Earth clears it). The HQ is pickable (`hq_picked`) and selectable, which sets `selected_planet = ENEMY_HOME` and shows the HQ inspector plus the shared rosters: fighters stationed at or bound for the HQ appear, are click and box selectable, and right-click recalls them, while `issue_group_order` skips mining drones for the HQ and `set_earth_rally` refuses it.
- Initial camera: `camera_target` is {10, 0, 2} (center of the planet extents) and `CAMERA_START_Y` (= 200 - 185 * 0.60) opens at 60% zoom, framing all eight planets beside the 330px inspector.
- Menus follow Star Fox Zero's options screen: `draw_menu_backdrop` dims the scene and draws a bottom bar of key-hint pills, `draw_menu_frame` draws the bracketed frame with side tabs and a title bar, and `draw_menu_item` draws full-width rows with a bottom-right notch and an arrow, filled solid green with dark text when focused, plus a small keycap for the shortcut. Mouse hover moves focus (`menu_hover_index` only reacts when the mouse moves or clicks, so it never fights the keyboard). Rects come from `menu_box` / `menu_item_rect` for both drawing and hit-testing.
- Start menu: `in_start_menu`, `start_menu_rects`, `update_start_menu`, `draw_start_menu`. Options are `NEW GAME` (N), `QUIT` (Q, also ESC), and `CONTINUE` (C) only when `save_game_exists()`. Arrow keys and ENTER navigate and activate; the mouse also clicks the rows.
- Pause on `P` or `F10` (`pause_key_pressed`; ESC does not pause). `PAUSED` menu with five rows: `CONTINUE` (C), `SAVE GAME` (S), `LOAD GAME` (L, disabled as `LOAD GAME - NO SAVE` when there is none), `NEW GAME` (N), `QUIT` (Q). Keyboard-navigable (`advance_pause_selection` wraps, ENTER calls `activate_pause_selection`), and `toggle_pause` resets focus to CONTINUE. `save_feedback_timer` swaps the title-bar subtitle to an amber `GAME SAVED TO DISK`. `F5` quick-saves during unpaused play and raises `hud_save_notification_timer`.
- End screens share `draw_end_menu` / `update_end_menu` / `end_menu_rects`: `SYSTEM LIBERATED` (green) offers `PLAY AGAIN` and `CRITICAL DEFEAT` (red accent, including the footer bar) offers `RESTART`; both run `restart_game` (`reset_world` plus `initialize_game`) via R, a click, or ENTER on the focused row, and `QUIT` via Q, ESC, or the row. UP/DOWN moves focus (`end_menu_selection`).

## Rendering

- `draw_world` (in `rendering_3d.odin`) draws the whole scene and falls back to flat `DrawSphere` when planet visuals are not initialized.
- Planets have no textures. `PLANET_FS` in `planet_visuals.odin` shades every pixel procedurally from 3D simplex fBm sampled at the object-space normal, so there are no UV seams or polar pinching and detail holds at any zoom. `planetKind` (the planet index) picks the look: cratered Mercury, swirling Venus, Earth with oceans, coasts, ice caps, and drifting clouds, Mars with caps, and banded gas giants with Jupiter's red storm and Neptune's dark spot. Lighting is a fixed `PLANET_LIGHT_DIR` with a soft terminator, ocean specular (alpha of `surface()` is the spec mask), and a Fresnel atmosphere rim tinted by `planet_atmospheres`. The `fog` uniform desaturates and darkens unscouted planets. Models are 48x48 `GenMeshSphere` spun by `planet_spin`, which advances in `step_simulation` via `update_planet_spin`, so spin freezes on pause. Cloud and band drift reads `laser_anim_time`, so it freezes too.
- Around each planet: a screen-space additive halo (`draw_planet_halos`, baked radial texture, drawn before the 3D pass so the sphere occludes its core), the drone-orbit ring, and for Saturn a banded, double-sided, alpha-blended ring mesh (`build_saturn_ring_model`, drawn without depth writes). Labels are centered below each planet (`planet_label_y`); the selected planet gets the broken three-arc cyan glow ring (`draw_planet_reticle`), red for the HQ. Conquered-planet badges anchor below the label, so labels and badges never overlap.
- Ambient nebulae (`ambient_nebulae`, `draw_ambient_nebulae`) are four billboards at fixed world anchors below the play plane, drawn additively in screen space behind the halos and combat nebulae. `bake_nebula_atlas` renders `NEBULA_BAKE_FS` once on the GPU into a mipmapped 2x2 atlas (`nebula_atlas`, one cloud per cell; R = gas density, G = hot glow), sharing `NOISE_GLSL` with `PLANET_FS`. `NEBULA_FS` tints R with the layer color and G with a whitened version. Each nebula draws two layers through `draw_nebula_layer`: its cell in `gas`, plus the opposite cell at `NEBULA_CORE_SCALE` in `core`, counter-rotating at twice the speed. All of it is one batch. `spin` (deg/s) runs on `laser_anim_time`, so keep it a multiple of 0.1 or the layers snap when that clock wraps at 3600. Colors stay violet, blue, teal, and magenta so they never read as the red combat or amber warning glows.
- SHARP EDGE: `DrawCircle3D` draws in the XY plane, so a ground-plane circle needs rotation axis `{1, 0, 0}` with 90 degrees. Rotating about `{0, 1, 0}` (the old code) leaves the circle standing vertically.
- SHARP EDGE: `draw_miner_drone`, `draw_fighter_drone`, and `draw_orbital_defense` draw nothing by themselves; they queue an instance. Any new 3D pass that draws them outside `draw_world` must call `flush_drones` / `flush_orbital_defenses` before `EndMode3D`, or they vanish (or leak into the next frame's batch).
- SHARP EDGE: `GenMeshSphere` generates vertices with the polar axis along Z. `init_planet_visuals` sets `model.transform = MatrixRotateX(DEG2RAD * -90)` so poles align to +Y (matching the `planet_spin` axis, `{0, 1, 0}`). The shader samples the untransformed `vertexNormal`, so latitude there is `p.z`, not `p.y`.
- The HQ (`hq_model.odin`) is baked once in `init_hq_visuals` into five static GPU models (`HQ_Model_Part`: intact hull, wreck hull, turret yaw block, gun, radar dish). `draw_hq_fortress` costs about 4 draw calls plus a handful of pulsing glow spheres, and `draw_world` skips it entirely when `hq_on_screen` says it is outside the viewport (the inspector panel counts as off-screen). Build geometry with the `hq_*` builder procs: `hq_slab` extrudes convex outlines (`hq_octagon`, `hq_chamfer_rect`) with flat normals, `hq_slab_windows` / `hq_slab_band` add lit window rows and glow strips on slab faces, and `hq_tri` fixes winding from the given normal because backface culling is on.
- `HQ_FS` lights the HQ with `PLANET_LIGHT_DIR` plus fill, sky, spec, and a red rim. Texcoords are material flags, not UVs: `x` = emissive (unlit), `y` = procedural panel plating in object space (seams fade out with `fwidth`, so no shimmer when zoomed out). Fog and wreck are shader uniforms (`fog` darkens and desaturates unlit parts; `wreck` turns everything to scorched grey with rare ember flickers), so there is one palette (`HQ_*` colors) and no per-state rebuild.
- The turret and gun rotate by writing an orthonormal basis into `model.transform` (`draw_hq_part`). Their pivot is `HQ_BATTERY_PIVOT` with a muzzle `HQ_MUZZLE_REACH` along the barrel, which `combat.odin` shares, so blasts leave the drawn muzzle. The sensor mast sits on the rear-left pod (`HQ_MAST_ROOT`), outside the barrels' sweep radius; anything tall placed within about 3.5 of the pivot will be clipped by the barrels. The wreck hull drops one pod, snaps the mast, and adds breach holes and floating debris.
- SHARP EDGE: `rl.TextFormat` returns a `cstring` into a small rotating pool of static buffers (4 in stock raylib), so a result is only valid until the next few `TextFormat` calls reuse its slot. Format and draw or copy it immediately, and never hold one across other `TextFormat` calls. This once clobbered the bottom HUD status line: `status` was captured before `draw_squad_hud()` but drawn after it, and each badge's `TextFormat` consumed a buffer, so the saved string was overwritten. The fix was to draw `status` before `draw_squad_hud()`.
- The backdrop is a deterministic starfield (`generate_stars`, `draw_starfield`, `STAR_COUNT` 280) of white dots in a wide disc below the play plane, on a fixed LCG seed independent of the gameplay RNG, drawn as screen-space `DrawCircleV` before the 3D pass so it sits behind everything. `draw_starfield` discards off-screen and NaN projections with `>=` bounds.
- Combat nebulae (`draw_combat_nebulae`, also screen space, drawn right before `BeginMode3D`): a red palpitating nebula behind sectors in combat (`sector_in_combat`, cached per sector in `sector_combat_state`) and behind the HQ. The 3D planet and fortress models occlude the core while `.ADDITIVE` crimson lobes expand into background space, palpitating on multi-harmonic breathing pulses and transitioning through `combat_nebula_intensity`. 12 asymmetric lobes across 3 tiers (4 + 5 + 3), with an ambient shroud, hot corona, and ionization filaments; radii, lobe spans, and viewport bounds are halved for a tight warzone envelope.
- Earth industry: while Earth has units in production or a base under construction, the planet shader lights the whole globe like night Earth from orbit (`city_lights` in `PLANET_FS`). Lights cover land only, clustered into metros, towns, and road networks with teal tech districts and welding sparks; clouds dim them, the sunlight dims and cools, and they glow brightest on the night side. Two uniforms drive it: `industry` (`earth_industry_intensity`, +1.4/s up, -0.9/s down, zero for every other planet) and `industryLevel` (`earth_industry_level`, active lines plus base construction over `MAX_BASES + 1`, eased), which spreads the lights from coastal cities to the whole land mass. Both advance in `step_simulation`. Keep `city_lights` free of `fwidth` (the caller passes the pixel footprint `pw`) so it can early-return on ocean, ice, and unlit land; that early-out plus skipping the land color on ocean pixels keeps the cost near +0.08ms GPU at max zoom-in at 3440x1440 on an RX 6700 XT.
- Lasers: `draw_combat_lasers` -> `draw_laser_bolt` draws two phased bolts per ray (`LASER_BOLT_SPEED` 14.0, `LASER_BOLT_LEN` 0.65) animated on `laser_anim_time`, which advances in `step_simulation` (and `update_start_menu`) so bolts freeze on pause. `SCIFI_CYAN` is player fire and `rl.RED` is enemy fire, mirroring `update_planet_combat` (dogfights, miner sweeps, base sieges).
- Drones: every unit is drawn individually (no grouping), all through `drone_model.odin`. `build_miner_hull` builds the miner (flatbed chassis, caged ore tank with a glowing ore band and hatch, cab, spotlight, four spider legs, striped drill, twin thrusters) and `build_fighter_hull` the fighter (armored cowl, ocular sensor, twin railgun pods, mandible struts, plasma thruster); with `detailed` both add greebles (raised panels, vents, coils, pistons, fins, antennas, beacons). `DRONE_FS` lights them like `HQ_FS` with a faction rim (`DRONE_RIM_PLAYER` / `DRONE_RIM_ENEMY`); texcoords are material flags (`x` = emissive, `y` = a `SURF_*` id: plated seams, brushed metal, hazard stripes, pulsing engine, flowing energy) and the procedural detail fades out with `fwidth`. `flush_drones` (once per frame in `draw_world`) issues one `DrawMeshInstanced` per `Drone_Model_Kind` and LOD, so unit count adds no draw calls; the miner bob is baked into its instance transform. Beyond `DRONE_LOD_DISTANCE` the plain hulls (`drone_lod_models`, roughly a quarter of the detailed triangle count) are drawn, and the roster icons are baked from them. `Prism` (`open` skips the end caps) and the `drone_*` builders sit on top of the shared `hq_*` helpers. Instances go through `instance_transform`, whose otherwise unused bottom-row `x` (`m3`) is a per-instance power-down flag (`fragDim`) that turns emissive parts to dark metal.
- Planet batteries reuse the drone shader and instancing: `orbital_defense_model.odin` bakes four `Battery_Part` meshes (BASE spun with `planet_spin`, TURRET on azimuth, GUN on azimuth plus elevation, and the elevation RAM, stretched along its axis to span turret and gun), so every battery on the map costs four draw calls in total. The gun mesh ends at `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_MUZZLE_REACH` from `ORBITAL_DEFENSE_PIVOT`, which the blasts share. While building or upgrading the parts are queued with the power-down flag and the hazard lights flash; a construction site draws the BASE rising out of the planet with build progress under the hologram effects. Effects are drawn after the instanced parts so their translucency blends over them. `SURF_BEACON` blinks the pad and mast lights.
- Conquered-planet overlay badges (`draw_conquered_planet_overlays` in `ui_primitives.odin`) project each liberated planet to screen and show orbital ammo `n/10` with a `+` buy button when affordable, miners `n/cap`, and fighters `n`. They run at 0.30 alpha unless the planet is selected, hide off-screen or behind the camera, and `pick_conquered_planet_overlay_ammo_button` hit-tests the `+` for both the selected and unselected layouts.
- Headless visual check: render into `rl.LoadRenderTexture` plus `rl.LoadImageFromTexture` and `rl.ExportImage` (no window server involved) and pass relative filenames, since raylib resolves them against the working directory. Convert with `ImageFormat(&img, .UNCOMPRESSED_R8G8B8)` before exporting, or translucent overlays leave alpha holes that read as a grey wash in the PNG. Window-compositor captures and `TakeScreenshot` are unreliable on this dev box (3440x1440 at 125% DPI).

## Save and load

- `save_game` / `load_game` write plain text `savegame.txt`, mirrored in the working directory and next to the executable (`os.get_executable_directory`), and it is gitignored.
- `serialize_game_state` / `deserialize_game_state` cover the version, minerals, drone speed level, autobuild mode and last mode, Earth rally, camera, selected planet, base counts and base construction, per-base production lines and pending queues, refineries, orbital defense per sector (level, HP, aim, ammo, and pending state), the wave and minor-wave clocks, the intel snapshot, and every unit including `mining_active` and `squad`. If you add persistent state, add it in both directions here.
- `save_game_exists` gates CONTINUE and LOAD GAME; `delete_save_game` also exists in `save_system.odin`.

## Performance

- Late-game frame-rate work for 1500+ units: `world_sector_spots` (`[SECTOR_COUNT]World_Sector_Spots`) lets `draw_world` collapse over 75 per-sector and per-side loops into 2 linear passes over `unit_count`, with `draw_combat_lasers` taking the pre-collected spots.
- `planet_mps` and `global_mps` do a single linear pass that early-breaks at `planet_mining_cap`, which removed the old O(N^2) scans in the HUD and inspector.
- `draw_inspector`, `box_select`, and `click_unit_tiles` precompute `unit_tile_y` and `enemy_tile_y` outside per-unit loops; `draw_squad_hud` tallies squad counts in one pass instead of nine; `update_intel` updates all lit planets in one pass; `has_vision` uses squared distances; and `sector_in_combat` exits at the first combatant pair and is cached in `sector_combat_state` for the nebulae.

## Window and frame pacing

- `main` sets `.VSYNC_HINT`, `.MSAA_4X_HINT`, `.WINDOW_HIGHDPI`, and `.WINDOW_RESIZABLE`, opens borderless at 1280x760, and binds `F11` and Alt+Enter to `ToggleBorderlessWindowed`. `SetExitKey(.KEY_NULL)` frees ESC for gameplay.
- SHARP EDGE: with `.VSYNC_HINT` enabled, never call `rl.SetTargetFPS(60)`. Raylib's `EndDrawing` limiter calls `WaitTime` (nanosleep) after `glfwSwapBuffers`, and on macOS and Windows kernel timer granularity oversleeps by 1-2ms, missing the 16.67ms VBLANK swap deadline and halving presentation to exactly 30 FPS. The code instead derives the cap from `GetMonitorRefreshRate` and calls `SetTargetFPS(max(refresh, 60) * 2)`, a safety ceiling that keeps `WaitTime` from sleeping during VSYNC-locked presentation.
